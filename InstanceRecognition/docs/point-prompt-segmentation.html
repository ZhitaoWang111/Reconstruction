GPT 点选 + SAM3：让 mask 粒度由“什么算一个物体”决定

现行感知阶段由 GPT 报类别名、SAM 按文本找实例，粒度完全交给 SAM，于是一整面橱柜被切成一块块门板。 点选方案把粒度交还给 GPT：GPT 先列出要分割的物体，再在图上点选正负点，SAM3 按点出 mask， GPT 看着渲染结果修正、组合、保留。本页记录这个方案的设计、它为什么这样设计，以及 7 帧小规模试验的结果。

00为什么改

文本 prompt 下，粒度是 SAM 决定的

现行流程（src/perception/）分两步：GPT 看一帧图，返回一组类别名（规则明确写着 “List a category once … SAM determines the instances; do not return counts, boxes, coordinates”）； SAM3.1 对每个类别名调用一次文本 prompt，返回它认为属于这个概念的所有实例，代码原样保留，不做合并。

问题出在“实例”的边界由 SAM 定。GPT 在帧 2794 正确地只写了一个 built-in cabinet， SAM 对这个短语返回了 8 个 mask——每对柜门一个；帧 2571 的 cabinet 返回了 11 个。 这就是 v14 里 Object064（电视柜）和 Object059（书柜墙）被拆碎的源头：下游拿到的观测本身就是门板。

文本 prompt 本身没“框错”：每块门板都是对的门板。错的是粒度——SAM 认为“cabinet”的一个实例是一组门，而我们需要的是整件家具。 再写一个更长的 sam_prompt 或事后合并同类 mask 都只是绕着这个问题走；点选让真正知道“什么算一个物体”的那一方来定边界。

01思路

GPT 定“分什么、分到多大”，SAM 只负责贴边

GPT
决定画面里有哪些物体、每个物体包含哪些部分（整面墙柜算一个，书单独算），并通过正负点把这个判断告诉 SAM。看渲染结果，判断 mask 是否覆盖整个物体且只覆盖它，不对就改点。
SAM3
把一组点变成一个贴着真实边缘的 mask。它擅长的是边缘，不需要它理解“一个物体”是什么。
宿主
渲染 GPT 看得懂的图（网格、拼图、总览）、计算 GPT 用眼看容易漏的事实（点是否落在 mask 内、碎块、面积门槛、重叠），在结束时检查每个计划都有着落。

这和 harness 的设计立场一致：几何和像素统计由宿主算好作为证据，判断交给模型。和“一次性让 GPT 吐出所有点”不同，这里是循环：点完立刻看结果再改，坐标估得不准可以在下一轮补救。

为什么是点，不是框

框能一次限定范围，但 GPT 估计像素框的误差直接变成 mask 的误差（框小了切掉物体，框大了吃进邻居）。点只要求“落在物体表面内部”，容错大得多； 多个点散布在各部件上就能表达“这些部件是一个整体”，负点能精确切掉某个邻居。trial3 里 GPT 一共打了 276 个正点，只有 6 个最终落在 mask 外。

02SAM 点提示的行为

三个探针实验，决定了 prompt 里的打点规则和宿主的细化次数

用哪个模型、哪个入口

现行流程用的 sam3.1_multiplex.pt 视频预测器，文本路径明确拒绝点（sam3_multiplex_tracking.py：“Point prompts not accepted”）。 试验改用同目录下的 sam3.pt 图像模型，以 build_sam3_image_model(enable_inst_interactivity=True) 构建， 调用 model.predict_inst(state, point_coords, point_labels, mask_input, multimask_output)。加载约 6 s，显存约 3.7 GB；每帧 set_image 一次，之后每组点都是毫秒级。

发现 1：一个点只给最小的一块

结论写进 prompt：多部件物体要在每个可见部件上各放一个正点，并覆盖到物体的四角。单点时宿主开 multimask_output，取得分最高的一个。

发现 2：要迭代细化

SAM 支持把上一次输出的低分辨率 logits 作为 mask_input 再喂回去。同一组 GPT 给的点：

细化遍数SAM 分数面积连通块数
10.3230.0%576
20.8137.9%160
30.8940.7%42

宿主固定做 3 遍（第 1 遍点 → 第 2、3 遍点 + 上一遍 logits），GPT 只看到最终结果。prompt 里告诉它这一点，免得它为“第一遍的碎块”去加点。

发现 3：负点要谨慎，框不划算

只给正点不给负点，3 遍后 mask 会漫到 60% 的画面（把书和墙都吃进去）；负点是必要的。 直接用框（由正点外接矩形外扩 60 px）只得到 0.61 分、26% 面积的残缺 mask——框不能替代点。

一帧一个对话；每轮恰好一个工具调用；默认上限 24 轮

输入

04工具设计

每个工具对应一个具体需要；宿主算的是 GPT 用眼看容易漏的

工具参数返回与设计理由
planobjects[{label, where}] 返回计划 id。先计划后分割有两个作用：迫使 GPT 先通盘扫一遍画面再动手（试验里漏物体主要发生在没扫边缘时）； 给 finish 一个可以核对的清单——每个计划都必须有着落，模型不能悄悄放弃一个难分的大物体。
segmentitems[{plan_id, positive[[x,y]…], negative[…]}]，≤6 项；正点 ≤20，负点 ≤8 一次分割多个物体以节省轮次。返回一张拼图（每个候选一块 tile：mask 着色、其余压暗、绿点 = 正点、红叉 = 负点；小物体自动裁剪放大）， 以及每个候选的面积占比、bbox、SAM 分数和警告：正点不在 mask 内、负点在 mask 内、碎成多块、面积低于下游门槛（0.5% 画面）、与已保留 mask 重叠 >10%。
zoombox [x0,y0,x1,y1] 返回放大到长边 1000 px 的裁剪，网格步长自适应（10–100 px），标签仍是原图坐标——GPT 不用做坐标换算。用于小物体和贴边打点。
combineplan_id, union[C…], subtract[C…] 对同一计划的多个候选做并集，可减去其他候选。为大物体分块分割而设：一组点盖不全整面墙柜时，上柜、中段、下柜分三次分，再合起来； 也用于把书架段从书柜 mask 里减掉。trial1 里没有这个工具，GPT 在单组点失败后直接跳过了整面墙柜。
keepcandidates[C…] 保留候选；同一计划后保留的覆盖先保留的。返回全帧已保留总览（大的先画、小的叠在上面，带计划 id 标签）、未保留计划列表、 以及已保留 mask 之间的重叠警告（小的有 >30% 在大的里面）——“书在书柜里”是合理的，“墙柜吃进了旁边的高柜”不是，由 GPT 判断。
finishskipped[{plan_id, reason}] 结束这一帧。任何既没保留也没跳过的计划都会被拒绝并点名；跳过必须写原因（试验里的原因如“只露出一条看不出是什么的边”）。

全文（trial3 版本）

You segment one RGB frame from a static indoor scan. You do it by clicking: you choose points on the image, a promptable segmenter (SAM) turns each set of points into one mask, you look at the result, and you keep the masks that are right. The goal is one mask per physical object that a 3D reconstruction could use.

## WHAT COUNTS AS ONE OBJECT

An object is one thing you could pick up, move or point to as a unit: a chair, a pillow, a backpack, a television, a bed.

Built-in and multi-part furniture is one object, not one per part. A cabinet with eight doors is one cabinet. A wall unit that combines cupboards and open shelves is one object. A desk with drawers is one desk. Doors, drawers, handles, shelves, legs and panels are never separate objects.

Things resting on or stored in furniture are separate objects when they are separate things: a lamp on a nightstand, a backpack on a chair. A shelf full of books is one "books" object per shelf section you can outline, not one per book.

Repeated identical objects are separate objects: four bar stools are four masks, six pillows are six masks. Matching footwear is one "pair of shoes" mask per pair.

Skip walls, floors, ceilings, and anything smaller than about 80 x 80 pixels: such masks are too small to reconstruct and are discarded downstream. Skip objects so cut off by the frame edge or so occluded that you cannot tell what they are. An object that is only partly in frame but recognisable — wall cabinets along the top edge, an appliance at the side — is still planned and segmented: its visible part is useful.

Scan the whole frame before planning: the top and side edges, the counter tops, the space under furniture. Objects there are easy to miss.

## THE IMAGE AND ITS COORDINATES

Coordinates are pixels of the original image: x from 0 at the left edge to the image width, y from 0 at the top edge to the image height. The first message gives you the frame twice: as it is, and with a labelled grid every 100 pixels. Read positions off the grid. `zoom` returns a magnified crop with a finer grid, still labelled in full-image pixels, for small objects and for placing points near edges.

## HOW SAM READS YOUR POINTS

A positive point says "this pixel belongs to the object"; a negative point says "this pixel does not".

One positive point is ambiguous. SAM answers with the smallest coherent region under it: click a cabinet door and you get that door; click a handle and you may get the handle. To get a whole multi-part object, put one positive point on every distinct visible part — each door, each shelf section, the frame between them — spread over the object's full extent, including near its corners.

Every positive point must lie on the object's visible surface, well inside it (at least 15 pixels from its edge). A point on a gap, on a reflection, or on whatever is in front of the object will pull in the wrong thing.

Use negative points to cut away leakage: if the mask spills onto a neighbouring object, add a negative point on that neighbour, well inside it.

The host refines each point set three times, so you see the refined mask. A mask below the downstream minimum size cannot be kept; skip that object instead.

## TOOLS

- `plan` lists the objects you intend to segment, each with a label and where it is. It returns plan ids (P1, P2, ...). You can call it again to add objects you missed.
- `segment` takes up to six planned objects, each with its points, and returns one candidate per object (C1, C2, ...) drawn on the frame with your points, plus per-candidate numbers and warnings: positive points the mask does not contain, negative points it does, overlap with masks you already kept.
- `combine` builds one candidate for a plan id from the union of several of its candidates, optionally subtracting others (for example a books candidate cut out of a bookcase candidate).
- `zoom` returns a magnified crop with a finer grid.
- `keep` accepts candidates. A later keep for the same plan id replaces the earlier one. It returns the frame with everything kept so far.
- `finish` ends the frame. Every plan id must be kept or listed in `skipped` with a reason.

## HOW TO WORK

1. Look at both images. Call `plan` with every object in the frame that meets the rules above. Use common, specific category names ("office chair", "nightstand", "wall cabinet"), the same name for the same kind of object, and English only.
2. Call `segment` on several planned objects at once. Large objects first, then the things in front of or on them.
3. Check every candidate against the image: does it cover the whole object and nothing else? For a multi-part object, go part by part over its visible extent — each door, each section, each end — and look for a part left dark in the tile; a missing part means a missing positive point there. Read the warnings. If a candidate is wrong, segment that plan id again with changed points — more positives on the missing parts, negatives on the leak. Do not keep a mask you would not accept as the object's outline.
4. `keep` the good ones. Read its overlap warnings — a large mask that swallowed a separate neighbour must be fixed, for example by `combine` with that neighbour's candidate subtracted. Look at the returned picture of all kept masks: an object with no mask on it is either still to do, or missing from your plan.
5. When every planned object is kept or skipped, call `finish`.

## WHEN ONE POINT SET IS NOT ENOUGH

Large multi-part objects sometimes come back with parts missing or with a leak that negative points do not remove. Then segment the object in pieces under the same plan id — for example the upper cupboards, the middle section and the lower cupboards as three `segment` items — and `combine` them. Do not skip a large object because a single point set failed: a combined mask covering the object is far more useful than none.

A mask does not have to be pixel-perfect. Keep it when it covers nearly all of the object and nearly nothing else; small ragged edges or a few missing patches along a border are acceptable. Reject it when a whole part is missing or when it covers a different object.

Each reply must contain exactly one tool call. Your budget is limited and the host warns you when two rounds remain; segmenting several objects per call and keeping several candidates per call is how you stay within it.

06三轮迭代

每轮只针对上一轮看到的具体失败改一处

trial1 → trial2：大物体被放弃 帧 2794

trial1 的 GPT 对墙柜试了 3 组点：一组缺上柜，一组漏进书和旁边的高柜，一组仍缺右侧。它在 finish 里写“Candidates either omit substantial cabinet sections or leak into books … no reliable complete mask”，跳过了整面墙柜——这恰恰是我们最需要的那个 mask。

改动：加 combine 工具；prompt 加“分块分割再合并”“不要因为一组点失败就跳过大物体”“不必像素级完美”。

trial1 → trial2：漏掉画面边缘的物体 帧 4471

trial1 只分了地柜、碗架、烤面包机三样，漏掉了上沿露出一截的吊柜和右侧的冰箱。

改动：prompt 加“部分出画但认得出的照样分”“规划前先扫四边、台面、家具底下”。

trial2 → trial3：大 mask 吞掉邻居

trial2 帧 2794 的墙柜 mask 把左边相邻的高柜一并吃进去，又单独保留了那个高柜，两个 mask 大面积重叠，GPT 没察觉——两者是在同一次 keep 里保留的，segment 的重叠警告只对“已保留”的生效。

改动：keep 返回已保留 mask 之间的两两重叠警告（小的 >30% 在大的里），措辞说明“书在书柜里是合理的，大 mask 漏到另一个物体上则要修”；prompt 第 4 步要求读这个警告。

trial3 里这个问题减轻但没根除：GPT 不再单独保留左侧高柜，但墙柜左缘仍带着高柜的一些碎片（见 09 节）。

09遗留问题与下一步

7 帧够看出方向，不够下结论

遗留问题

  • 大 mask 的边缘越界：帧 2794 的墙柜左缘带着相邻高柜的碎片，帧 3018 的沙发下缘带少量地面。重叠警告只覆盖“两个都保留了”的情况；漏到一个没被计划的东西上，宿主看不出来，只能靠 GPT 看图。可以考虑的宿主侧手段：报告 mask 落在画面边缘之外 / 与 bbox 填充率异常的部分，或对大 mask 做一次“只保留包含正点的连通块”。
  • 跨帧一致性：每帧独立，同一件家具这帧叫 wall unit、那帧叫 bookcase，切分方式也可能不同（帧 2571 把一组家具分成电视柜 / 墙柜 / 高柜三件，别的视角未必同样分）。harness 不依赖标签，但切分不一致会在跨帧关联时制造“部分对整体”的情况。
  • 小物体漏检：每帧由 GPT 一次规划决定分什么，规划漏掉的物体就没有 mask（帧 3018 漏了两个遥控器）。现行流程由 SAM 按类别名搜全图，同类小物体不容易漏。
  • 规模：只有 7 帧，没跑完 lift → harness，不知道最终 Object 的质量变化。
  • 模型与环境：试验用的是 sam3.pt 图像模型，主流程用的是 sam3.1_multiplex.pt；两份权重共存，接入时需要定下来用哪个。

建议的下一步

  1. 用 trial3 的 prompt 跑完 100 帧（与 scene_amb3r_full100_v1 相同的帧），生成对比页人工抽查。
  2. 把输出写成与现行 predictions.json 相同的结构，复用 assemble_perception_source → amb3r_mask_source 生成 source，跑一次 harness，与 v14 对比 Object064 / Object059 及整体物体数。
  3. 效果确认后，再把 tmp/point_prompt/run.py 正式化为 src/perception/ 下的一条新路径（保留现行文本路径作对照）。
